Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how the TikTok Affiliate Uploader application ("the App") handles information when you use it to connect and publish content to your own TikTok account.

1. Who operates this app

This App is operated and used solely by its owner () as a personal tool for managing their own TikTok content. It is not offered as a public service to other users.

2. Information we access

When you authorize this App via TikTok Login Kit, we access:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name) via the user.info.basic scope.
  • The ability to publish videos directly to your TikTok profile via the video.publish scope.

We do not access your TikTok password. Authentication is handled entirely by TikTok's own OAuth 2.0 login page.

3. How information is stored

Access tokens and refresh tokens obtained from TikTok are stored locally on the owner's own device, in a local file that is never transmitted to any third-party server. Tokens are used only to make authorized API calls directly to TikTok's own servers (open.tiktokapis.com).

4. How information is used

Information obtained from TikTok is used exclusively to authenticate the account owner and to publish videos the owner explicitly selects to TikTok, with the caption and settings the owner explicitly enters in the App before each publish action.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained from TikTok with any third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used within this App.

6. Data retention and deletion

Stored tokens remain on the local device until the owner disconnects the account from within the App, or manually deletes the local token file. You may revoke this App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings under "Manage Third-Party Apps."

7. Contact
